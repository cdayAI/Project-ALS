## 1. Audit verdict

The central conclusion in `research/02_ai_methods.md` is directionally sound: AI is most useful in ALS as a prioritization, measurement, retrieval, and engineering layer; it does not turn correlational data or a virtual score into therapeutic evidence.

The brief is not yet reliable as a current evidence ledger. Its header says the literature is current through 2025 while it cites 2026 publications. Several examples are described as “wins” even though they demonstrate feasibility rather than clinical benefit. Several fast-moving method classes and major 2026 ALS datasets are omitted. The corrections below should govern future digests and investment decisions even if the original narrative remains unchanged.

### Live hypothesis-ledger preflight

The H-001–H-010 batch added to `origin/main` during this audit is an idea ledger, not an evidence upgrade. Three entries need correction before implementation:

- H-007 describes WVE-004's benefit as merely unproven. FOCUS-C9 was terminated after target engagement failed to correlate with functional outcomes and no clinical benefit was seen at 24 weeks ([ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04931862](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04931862)). That failure is calibration evidence, not an omitted uncertainty.
- H-008 proposes using [PRO-ACT](https://ncri1.partners.org/ProACT/) progression-rate surrogates where NfL is unavailable. Simulated clinical proxies cannot validate an NfL-trajectory model or establish the claimed 25% sample-size reduction; a linked longitudinal NfL/outcomes dataset is required. Verify whether NfL exists against an archived, release-specific PRO-ACT data dictionary rather than inferring variable absence from the live portal.
- H-009 proposes training molecular subtypes in Answer ALS and regressing them onto PRO-ACT trajectories. [Answer ALS](https://app-wp-prod-001.azurewebsites.net/data-information/) links its own omics and clinical records by participant ID, but PRO-ACT is a separate cohort without Answer ALS molecular labels. The proposed cross-repository regression therefore cannot directly test whether molecular subtype aligns with clinical progression stratum; a two-stage clinical-proxy study would be a different, weaker estimand.

The H-008 and H-009 issues are design non-identifiability problems, not details to solve with a more complex model; H-007 requires evidence calibration.

### Current implementation delta

The H-008 framework is appropriately and repeatedly labeled `SIMULATED_ONLY`, with real PRO-ACT and longitudinal NfL data still pending. Its own preregistered comparison is not met: at an assumed treatment effect of 0.30 ALSFRS-R points/month, the interpolated estimate of `n` per arm at 80% power falls from 34.4 under `clinical_static` to 28.7 under `nfl_ml`, a 16.6% reduction—not the claimed 25%. The 28.6% reduction from 40.2 to 28.7 is only versus unenriched enrollment, a weaker comparator. The reported NfL-slope `p < 1e-90` is generated by construction. Half of the screening pool also receives in-sample predictions before all subjects compete for selection, so the apparent enrichment gain is contaminated; use an independent training cohort or full cross-fitting. The written controls require noise `|delta| ≤ 5%` and oracle dominance over every learned arm, while code permits noise reduction down to -10% and never asserts dominance. F2's promised per-patient-benefit gate is not emitted and cannot be learned from a simulator that fixes mean treatment benefit by construction. The “none” arm still discards a random half and is reported as two screened per enrolled, so it is an unenriched random subset rather than operational no-screening enrollment. These outputs validate plumbing only, not H-008 or an ALS effect.

The trial matcher is the best-governed implemented stream: code and an independent review are on main; it retrieves live ClinicalTrials.gov records, cross-checks structured country locations, quotes length-limited source text for detected mutation/stage cues, distinguishes API failure from zero results, and refuses to decide eligibility. A live SOD1 run retrieved 71 records and surfaced the documented negation problem safely as quoted context. However, `--limit 5 --json` returned all 71 records (204,441 bytes) because the limit is applied only in text mode. `max_pages` can also stop while a `nextPageToken` remains despite the function's completeness promise, country confirmation uses substring matching, ties inherit live API order, and no response snapshot or API-version manifest is stored. It is a bounded retrieval prototype, not a reproducible eligibility engine.

The merged H-007 review supports only a narrow statement: the preregistered nucleolar/speckle module, **as computed from the two-pair GSE303931 experiment**, failed its enrichment bar. It explicitly makes the GSE283507 arm non-evidentiary because the gene universe and GO sets collapsed, variance was pathological, and no producing code was committed. This is not evidence that C9orf72 neurons lack nucleolar-stress biology. ## 2. Claims requiring calibration

| Topic | Current implication | Evidence-calibrated interpretation | Required gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drug-target Mendelian randomization | Genetic perturbation “mimics” the drug mechanism | A germline proxy reflects a small lifelong perturbation, not adult dosing, modality, tissue exposure, reversibility, or timing. MR can support causal direction but does not estimate an ALS treatment effect directly. | Fine-mapping, colocalization, tissue-relevant cis-QTLs, pleiotropy checks, direction consistency, and experimental triangulation. |
| Genetics and clinical success | Genetics roughly doubles success and therefore identifies the best targets | Across therapeutic areas, drug mechanisms with genetic support had a 2.6-fold higher probability of clinical success in an observational analysis. This is enrichment across programs, not a guarantee for an individual ALS target. | Preserve causal-gene uncertainty and report absolute as well as relative evidence. |
| Ropinirole | Concrete ALS AI-era treatment win | It is a legitimate iPSC-discovery-to-trial feasibility example. In 20 participants, the double-blind period did not show an ALSFRS-R difference; later favorable estimates came from a small, high-attrition open-label extension and cannot establish efficacy. | Independent, adequately powered randomized confirmation and prespecified responder analysis. |
| Tofersen and NfL | AI-adjacent biomarker-model win that changed practice | Tofersen is a rational ASO/genetics program, not an AI discovery. VALOR missed its 28-week clinical primary endpoint. FDA accelerated approval used plasma NfL as reasonably likely to predict benefit specifically in SOD1-ALS; this does not establish NfL as a universal ALS surrogate. | Separate target engagement, biomarker response, and clinical benefit; retain the confirmatory-trial requirement. |
| PRO-ACT | More than 8,000 placebo-arm patients | The current portal reports 13,717 anonymized records from 44 Phase II/III trials and includes active and placebo cohorts. The DREAM work used a selected subset; sample-size reductions are retrospective estimates, not prospective validation. | Dataset/version manifest, cohort-selection diagram, external temporal/registry validation, and prospective trial evidence before operational claims. |
| ENCALS/prognostic ML | Prospectively used in clinics and trials; ML cuts sample size | ENCALS is externally validated for survival prediction, but its original validation does not prove prospective treatment-selection benefit. Retrospective covariate simulations can estimate power gains; they do not demonstrate them in a completed prospective ALS trial. | Calibration by site/time, decision-impact study, missing-data handling, and prospective protocol inclusion. |
| Molecular subtypes | Ready substrate for targeted enrollment | Expression-defined clusters have been assigned across postmortem cortex and blood datasets, but they remain exploratory. They are not prospectively validated treatment-response strata and may reflect region, disease stage, or cell composition. | Reproducible assignment in premortem samples, locked classifier, external cohorts, and interaction with treatment—not prognosis alone. |
| TDP-43/FUS structure | They are intrinsically disordered proteins, making static prediction meaningless | Both are multidomain proteins with structured RNA-binding domains and intrinsically disordered low-complexity regions. Low pLDDT often tracks disorder but can also represent model uncertainty. In two ALS-FTLD donors, the pathological TDP-43 fold showed no similarity to the in-vitro TDP-43 filament structures compared in that paper. | Restrict structure-led design to a defined structured domain/epitope or experimentally constrained ensemble; require orthogonal binding and functional assays. |
| Boltz-2/DiffDock | Leading open virtual-screen stack with affinity throughput | These are promising engineering tools, not established ALS target-validation systems. Pose plausibility, hidden benchmark bias, protein conformation, novel-sequence generalization, and affinity calibration remain material limitations. | PoseBusters/physics checks, experimental structures or ligand constraints, classical docking baseline, prospective biochemical assay. |
| Speech/wearables | Strong digital trial endpoints | Current ALS studies support feasibility and responsiveness in selected settings. They do not yet establish treatment-effect validity, general reliability across devices/populations, or universal primary-endpoint qualification. | Device/language/site/stage validation, adherence and missingness analysis, test-retest reliability, longitudinal calibration, treatment-sensitivity study. |
| Rentosertib | First fully generative-AI target-plus-drug clinical win | It is a useful non-CNS Phase 2a case study. “AI-discovered” attribution is partly developer-defined, and a 12-week secondary FVC signal in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis does not demonstrate improved CNS or ALS success. | Treat as external feasibility context, not ALS validation or proof that AI caused the result. |

### Primary, official, and methodological evidence for these corrections

- Drug-target MR estimates lifelong genetic perturbation and is not directly comparable to a time-limited drug intervention ([Gill et al., 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7903200/), PMID 33644404); common instrument and tissue pitfalls are summarized with worked examples in [PMID 39407214](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39407214/).
- Genetic support was associated with 2.6-fold greater program-level clinical success in a cross-therapeutic observational analysis ([Minikel et al., 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11096124/), PMID 38632401).
- The ropinirole trial's double-blind ALSFRS-R result was not different from placebo, and the authors identify small size and open-label attrition as limitations ([Morimoto et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37267913/), PMID 37267913).
- VALOR showed biomarker engagement but missed its clinical primary endpoint ([Miller et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36129998/), PMID 36129998). FDA describes a SOD1-specific accelerated approval based on NfL; its current tracker lists the required ATLAS postmarketing trial as ongoing, with projected completion June 30, 2028 ([FDA Qalsody decision](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/fda-approves-treatment-amyotrophic-lateral-sclerosis-associated-mutation-sod1-gene); [accelerated-approval tracker](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/accelerated-approval-program/ongoing-non-malignant-hematological-neurological-and-other-disorder-indications-accelerated)).
- The live [PRO-ACT portal](https://ncri1.partners.org/ProACT/) reports 13,717 records, 44 trials, and both active and placebo cohorts as of this audit. The DREAM paper used 1,822 selected patients and estimated at least 20% sample reduction ([Küffner et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25362243/), PMID 25362243); a later externally tested model produced a simulated 16% effective-sample-size gain under a hypothetical treatment effect ([Zhou et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32862509/), PMID 32862509). Neither is prospective proof of trial efficiency.
- The ENCALS model was externally validated across European cohorts with a c-statistic of 0.78, but the paper is a prognostic-model study, not a prospective treatment-impact trial ([Westeneng et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29598923/), PMID 29598923).
- The molecular-subtype study discovered postmortem motor-cortex clusters and assigned them in other cortex and blood datasets, but did not validate treatment response ([Marriott et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38129934/), PMID 38129934).
- Patient-derived TDP-43 filament structures provide the relevant pathological constraint ([Arseni et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34880495/), PMID 34880495). The [AlphaFold DB limitations](https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/faq) explicitly distinguish confidence from experimental validation.
- PoseBusters found physically implausible poses and weak novel-sequence generalization across common deep-learning docking outputs ([Buttenschoen et al., 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10901501/), PMID 38425520). Separate studies document hidden DUD-E benchmark bias ([PMID 31430292](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31430292/)) and limitations of docking against AlphaFold receptor models ([PMID 36686396](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36686396/)). Boltz-2's original method remains a [2025 preprint](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40667369/); independent evaluations report task-dependent behavior rather than uniform affinity reliability ([PMID 42579383](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42579383/); [PMID 41592323](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41592323/)).
- Speech data from a vendor-authored observational study (278 participants recorded; the abstract reports responsiveness analyses in 36 bulbar-onset and 107 non-bulbar-onset participants) support responsiveness ([PMID 39126786](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39126786/)). Wearable/smartphone work supports feasibility and association with change in a 40-participant cohort ([PMID 36879025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36879025/)). Neither establishes general test-retest reliability, treatment-effect sensitivity, or endpoint qualification.
- The randomized rentosertib Phase 2a study enrolled 71 participants for 12 weeks; safety was primary and FVC was secondary ([PMID 40461817](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40461817/)). It is non-CNS feasibility evidence, not ALS validation or causal proof that AI improved development success.

## 3. Important method classes and evidence missing from the brief

### 3.1 Provenance-first literature mining and evidence graphs

The project needs a reproducible evidence layer before it needs an autonomous “hypothesis agent.” Use PubTator 3.0 for entity/relation extraction and Open Targets for traceable target-disease evidence. Preserve the exact sentence, source, date, species/model, evidence type, direction, intervention, outcome, and whether the result was positive or negative.

- [PubTator 3.0](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38572754/) exposes current biomedical annotations and relations (PMID 38572754).
- The [2025 Open Targets Platform paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39657122/) documents its evidence integration and target-prioritization interface (PMID 39657122).
- Hetionet is a useful open knowledge-graph precedent, and its authors explicitly controlled node degree with degree-weighted paths and degree-preserving permutations. Even so, connectivity is not causal evidence ([Himmelstein et al., 2017](https://elifesciences.org/articles/26726)).

Required validation: temporal holdouts, source-independent validation, explicit negative evidence, degree-matched baselines, and manual verification of every promoted edge. An LLM summary without retrieved primary evidence is a drafting aid, not repository evidence. For live resources—including PRO-ACT, ClinGen, PubTator, AlphaFold DB, GEO, and ClinicalTrials.gov—record `retrieved_at`, query, and response/snapshot hash in the run manifest, plus provider release/API version and returned provider timestamp where available.

### 3.2 Human genetics before drug-target MR

MR should follow foundational association, fine-mapping, and gene-validity work rather than replace it.

- A cross-ancestry ALS GWAS analyzed 29,612 cases and 122,656 controls and reported 15 risk loci ([van Rheenen et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34873335/), PMID 34873335).
- Protective IL18RAP 3′UTR variants are an unusually strong genotype-to-expression-to-mechanism example ([Eitan et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35361972/), PMID 35361972).
- A 2026 exome study of 17,919 cases and 200,703 controls added and strengthened rare-variant gene evidence ([PMID 41917433](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41917433/)).
- Use ClinGen gene-centric search and Gene Curation Expert Panel exports across ALS-spectrum disease terms to bound gene-disease validity. The [generic ALS condition page](https://search.clinicalgenome.org/kb/conditions/MONDO%3A0004976/by-gene) is not a complete ALS gene set; canonical genes may be curated under narrower terms, as the [SOD1 record](https://search.clinicalgenome.org/kb/genes/SOD1) illustrates.

The new `data/inventory/gwas_catalog_als_loci.json` is a useful raw association inventory, not evidence that Project ALS has identified 56 validated loci or causal genes. Its 56 author-reported gene labels cover 97 gene–association rows, but only 37 rows have `p ≤ 5e-8`; 60 are weaker, extending to `p = 9e-6`. The file maps single variants to multiple author-reported genes, includes `intergenic` as a label, leaves title/date/journal blank for all 28 studies, and records that its EFO query returned zero. Before it feeds H-003/H-004/H-006, preserve the raw API response and hash, complete study/ancestry/sample-overlap metadata, record effect allele/size/SE, genome build and mapping rule, distinguish discovery from replication, apply a prespecified significance tier, and fine-map/colocalize rather than equate a locus label with a causal drug target.

Executable target scoring should keep association strength, causal-gene probability, inheritance/model, tissue expression, mechanism direction, perturbation evidence, druggability, and safety liabilities as separate columns. Do not collapse them into one unexplained “AI score.”

### 3.3 Selected current ALS single-cell and spatial omics

The brief uses Alzheimer’s examples where current ALS-specific data now exist.

- A 2026 immune-focused discovery resource combines PBMC single-cell data from 22 participants with ALS and 18 controls—including only 4 C9-ALS participants—with bulk CNS data and spinal spatial profiling ([PMID 42135512](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42135512/)). The PBMC scRNA-seq accession is [GSE268995](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE268995); the spatial accession is [GSE288365](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE288365). Treat it as an immune-focused discovery resource, not a comprehensive ALS cell-state atlas.
- A joint snRNA-seq/ATAC-seq ALS study includes many nuclei but only six ALS and six control donors; donor, not nucleus, is the inferential unit ([PMID 41218062](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41218062/)).
- Donor-aware pseudobulk is a first-line benchmark for replicated cell-type differential expression because cell-level tests can inflate false discoveries ([Squair et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34584091/), PMID 34584091).

Separate cell-composition shifts from within-cell-state changes. Require donor-level inference, site/region/sex/age/PMI/RIN covariates where estimable, and an independent donor cohort before promoting a cell state to a therapeutic target.

### 3.4 Multimodal integration

MOFA+, DIABLO, totalVI/MultiVI, and related models are useful, but no integration method is a universal winner. The relevant test is whether multimodal integration beats the best single modality under grouped, nested, external validation—not whether a latent-space plot looks clean.

- [MOFA+](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32393329/) is an unsupervised latent-factor baseline (PMID 32393329); [DIABLO](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30657866/) is supervised and outcome-driven (PMID 30657866). They answer different questions and are not interchangeable.
- [MultiVI](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37386189/) jointly models chromatin and transcriptome measurements (PMID 37386189).
- A large **single-cell** multimodal benchmark across RNA, ADT, ATAC, and spatial tasks shows substantial method-, metric-, dataset-, and task-dependence; it does not validate bulk patient-level multiomics or ALS outcome prediction ([DOI 10.1038/s41592-025-02856-3](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02856-3)).

Split by donor, site, and cohort. Any preprocessing, feature selection, imputation, and hyperparameter choice must be fitted inside the training fold. Compare against clinical-only and single-omics baselines with calibration and uncertainty, not AUROC alone.

### 3.5 Protein language models

Protein language models add sequence-level representations, homolog discovery, missense-effect prioritization, and fast structure hypotheses:

- ESM sequence representations ([Rives et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33876751/), PMID 33876751);
- ESMFold structure prediction ([Lin et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36927031/), PMID 36927031);
- ESM1b-style variant-effect prediction ([PMID 37563329](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37563329/)).

For ALS, PLM scores can prioritize missense variants for assay, but they cannot establish pathogenicity or therapeutic direction. Scores can be computed for individual isoforms, but the model does not identify which isoform is disease-relevant; it also does not determine gain- versus loss-of-function or directly model repeat expansions, splicing/nonsense-mediated decay, transcript abundance, or condensate context. Record the exact model/checkpoint/version. Use ClinGen for gene-disease validity and [ClinVar](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/) records with expert-panel or practice-guideline review status where available, interpreted under the relevant ACMG/AMP framework alongside population frequency, segregation, molecular assays, and calibrated disease-gene-specific baselines.

### 3.6 ASO design needs failure calibration

The current brief emphasizes design predictors but omits the strongest ALS counterexamples.

- BIIB078 showed CSF poly(GP)/poly(GA) target engagement but no NfL reduction or clinical benefit. At 90 mg, NfL rose and clinical measures declined numerically versus placebo; these secondary/exploratory signals do not prove drug-caused harm, but development stopped ([PMID 39059407](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39059407/)).
- Jacifusen/FUS evidence remains uncontrolled and small; it is not a validated design benchmark ([PMID 40414239](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40414239/)).
- A 2026 study curated 59,273 gapmers: gene-specific models were strong, while standard cross-target models failed leave-one-gene-out generalization. Its ASO-RASAR read-across method partly rescued performance in simulated low-data settings and one WFDC1 target-gene validation experiment; that is not broad external-gene validation ([PMID 42364111](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42364111/)).

Public models may support target/isoform mapping, chemistry-property prediction, and off-target triage. They should not autonomously emit clinical sequences. Require isoform specificity, human transcriptome off-target analysis, chemistry and exposure constraints, immunostimulation/hepatotoxicity screening, and wet-lab potency/selectivity assays.

### 3.7 Trial matching is retrieval plus clinician-confirmed screening

The repository now contains an implemented and independently reviewed trial-retrieval prototype. It currently normalizes structured location, recruitment status, phase/study type, ages, sponsor, and enrollment, then adds small keyword-based mutation/stage scores and notes. It does not reliably normalize onset/duration, respiratory function, medications, genetic criteria, or exclusions, and it does not emit categorical match/exclusion decisions. Treat the following as the **future hardening contract**, not a description of completed behavior:

1. retrieve weekday-refreshed records from the [ClinicalTrials.gov API v2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/data-about-studies/learn-about-api) and record the `/api/v2/version` `dataTimestamp`;
2. preserve the structured fields already extracted and add explicit, independently tested handling for onset/duration, respiratory function, medications, genetic criteria, and exclusion text;
3. return ranked candidate records, explicit missing-information flags, and verbatim criterion excerpts with retrieval time; never infer exclusion from keyword heuristics;
4. require the trial site to determine eligibility.

The current prototype's “every run hits the live API; no local data is stored” policy is not by itself reproducible. Save the exact parameters, `retrieved_at`, provider `dataTimestamp` where available, returned NCT IDs, response hash, code commit, and a governed raw snapshot outside Git (or another immutable source reference sufficient to reconstruct the response).

TrialGPT demonstrates criterion-level retrieval and explanation, but its published evaluation used 183 synthetic patient notes with more than 75,000 annotations plus an NCI pilot; it is not prospective ALS clinical validation or evidence for autonomous eligibility decisions ([PMID 39557832](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39557832/)). Use synthetic or properly governed deidentified cases during development; never publish patient data or make an autonomous eligibility decision.

## 4. Method-class maturity matrix

This table describes methods mature enough to implement under the stated gates; it is not a claim that Project ALS has implemented them correctly.

| Tier | Method | Permitted output if correctly implemented | What it must not claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mature to implement** | Provenance-first literature retrieval; genetics evidence graph; donor-aware bulk/single-cell analysis; simple calibrated prognostic baselines; rule-grounded trial retrieval | Auditable evidence tables, replicated associations, uncertainty, ranked trial candidate records | Causality from co-occurrence, treatment advice, final trial eligibility |
| **Ready with gates** | Drug-target MR; multimodal integration; CMap reversal; PLM variant scoring; structured-domain docking; ASO property models | Ranked hypotheses with sensitivity analyses and explicit failure modes | Efficacy, pathogenicity, binding, BBB exposure, or safety without orthogonal evidence |
| **Premature for decisions** | Treatment-response molecular subtypes; small-`n` deep clinical prediction; autonomous eligibility; docking TDP-43/FUS low-complexity regions; unvalidated generative CNS candidates as decision-grade evidence; clinical ASO sequence generation | Research prototypes and negative benchmarks | Patient stratification, therapeutic selection, “validated hit,” or medical advice |

Simple baselines are mandatory in every tier. A foundation model or graph neural network that does not beat regularized regression, pathway scores, nearest-neighbor retrieval, or a prespecified clinical model under external validation adds complexity, not evidence.

“Ready with gates” for CMap means cell-line-, dose-, time-, and context-dependent hypothesis generation under the contract in `research/05_pilot_methods_validity.md`, not evidence that a disease state has been corrected ([Subramanian et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29195078/), PMID 29195078).

## 5. Recommended research sequence

1. Apply the donor-aware and CMap validity contract in `research/05_pilot_methods_validity.md` before any exp001b retry; do not interpret the existing exp001 ranks.
2. Build a provenance-first ALS evidence table joining ClinGen, the 2021 GWAS, 2026 exomes, Open Targets, PubTator, and current human cell-state resources.
3. Score targets transparently by separate evidence dimensions; nominate a small set for causal triangulation rather than one opaque global rank.
4. Reanalyze public omics with donor-level inference and a locked independent cohort. Treat “no replicated signal” as a successful falsification result.
5. Harden the existing trial matcher with frozen synthetic regression profiles, a versioned live-response manifest, and site-confirmed eligibility boundaries; keep keyword scores advisory and source-quoted.
6. Use PLMs and structure models only after a target survives genetics, expression, mechanism, tractability, and assay-availability gates.
7. Benchmark every complex model against a simple baseline and publish negative results, missing data, and external dependencies.

This sequence funds the measurable wins first: evidence quality, causal prioritization, reproducible inference, and patient-facing retrieval. It defers high-cost generative work until a target and validation assay justify it.
