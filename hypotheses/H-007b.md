## Rationale
H-007 predicted a nucleolar-stress/speckle DPR correlate; it was killed on its own pre-registered bar (depleted in GSE303931 across every module definition). What the source paper itself reported, and what motivated this replacement, is a cytoskeletal organization / ECM adhesion / synaptic signaling signature with a concrete mechanistic anchor: FLNB exon-30 skipping, loss of the hinge domain, disrupted actin crosslinking and mechano-transduction (PMID 42221822). A cytoskeletal/ECM module is pharmacologically tractable and carries a built-in PD readout (module-score reduction plus FLNB exon-30 splicing restoration, the latter shown responsive to ropinirole, PMID 40826812).

## Threshold derivation (pre-analysis, from literature not from our data)
- The 2-fold overlap-enrichment bar is retained unchanged from the H-007 standard that the adversarial reviewer endorsed ("keep the >=2-fold standard").
- Direction-specificity (down-regulated side only) follows the replicated signal direction in PMID 42221822 (loss of cytoskeletal/ECM gene expression with FLNB loss of function).
- Conjunctive single-primary-family design avoids the disjunctive-large-set rigging failure mode identified in review sec 4a.
- Permutation-based significance with size-matched random draws follows standard competitive gene-set testing logic (Subramanian et al., PNAS 2005, PMID 16199517; we use overlap-permutation instead of KS for transparency, with the empirical null distribution reported).
- Count-level reprocessing requirement follows the reviewer's finding that deposited TPMs carry near-zero within-group variance (median within-group log2TPM var ~0.019), invalidating parametric P values.
