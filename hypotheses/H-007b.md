## Rationale
H-007 predicted the C9orf72 iPSC transcriptomic correlate would concentrate on nucleolar stress and nuclear speckles. Direct testing falsified that: both gene-set families fail the pre-registered >=2-fold enrichment bar in GSE303931 (depleted) and GSE283507 (neutral/sub-bar). What replicates instead is the module class the source paper itself reported: cytoskeletal organization, ECM adhesion, synaptic signaling, with a concrete mechanistic anchor (FLNB hinge-domain loss, impaired mechano-transduction). A cytoskeletal/ECM module is pharmacologically tractable (cytoskeleton/ECM/integrin nodes are druggable) and carries a built-in PD readout (module-score reduction plus FLNB exon-30 splicing restoration, the latter already shown responsive to ropinirole in PMID 40826812). This hypothesis reuses the H-007 method chain - genotype-defined signature -> LINCS reversal -> annotated candidates with CNS-penetration filters - against the module that actually survives.
