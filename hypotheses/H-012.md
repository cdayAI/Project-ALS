## Rationale
H-007's nucleolar/speckle module died, but its underlying literature anchor - G4C2 RNA disrupting speckles and splicing integrity (PMID 39181135) - was never actually wrong; it was tested in a dataset too small to measure anything. AnswerALS published exactly this phenotype at portal scale (significant skipped-exon/intron-retention enrichment in C9 vs controls). Splicing load has three properties worth a hypothesis: it is (a) mechanistically proximal to both G4C2 RNA toxicity and TDP-43 dysfunction, (b) quantifiable per donor as a single scalar (PC1 of residual intron excision), which converts an underpowered many-gene problem into a properly powered few-parameter problem, and (c) intervention-responsive (ropinirole precedent), making it a credible pharmacodynamic readout for DPR-directed trials - the trial-ready signature H-007 originally promised.

## Threshold derivation (pre-analysis)
Two-stage design because no local PSI variance exists: the pilot gate measures SD first;
primary-test MDEs are then fixed arithmetic (0.65-0.75 SD_PC1 at n=15-20/group; beta>=0.45
for the repeat-length gradient at n~41 carriers). The go/no-go gates are written so the
hypothesis dies cheaply if variance is worse than assumed, instead of producing another
artifact-driven verdict.
