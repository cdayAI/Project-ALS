## Rationale
Druggable-genome MR has produced convergent, independent nominations of TBK1 for ALS (Duan et al., PMID 38443977), and TBK1 is also a bona fide monogenic ALS gene - rare human genetics and population MR rarely agree this cleanly. But TBK1 LOF causes ALS, so a naive 'inhibit the MR hit' readout is suspect. We hypothesize the apparent conflict resolves by cell type: risk alleles act through reduced TBK1 in one compartment (consistent with haploinsufficiency) while MR instruments tag expression changes in another, and that colocalization plus Answer ALS cell-type-resolved expression can adjudicate direction-of-effect. This yields either (a) an activation/up-regulation strategy (e.g., TBK1 stimulators) or (b) a clean demotion of the MR signal as pleiotropic - both informative outcomes.
