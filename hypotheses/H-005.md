## Rationale
Pathologic TDP-43 is hyperphosphorylated in essentially every ALS case regardless of upstream cause (PMID 17023659, 40252666), making the phosphorylation machinery a shared-node therapeutic surface. Kinases such as casein kinase 1 family and TTBK1/TTBK2 have been implicated in tau/TDP phosphorylation biology, and condensate physics respond sharply to PTM state (PMIDs 28790177, 32132204). The testable computational claim: build a TDP-43 phosphorylation/aggregation-associated gene module from open multi-omics, then screen ALL LINCS genetic perturbation signatures (not just drugs) for reversal. Kinases whose knockdown reverses the module are candidate anti-pTDP-43 targets; phosphatases whose overexpression does the same are the complementary arm. This is deliberately cheap, fully falsifiable, and hands a ranked list to any future iPSC-motor-neuron phospho-assay.
