## Rationale
The bexarotene collapse (spectacular systems story, failed replication within a year; PMIDs 23764200, 26025659) and the clemastine success (signature fused to functional assay) bracket what signature reversal can do. Between them sits an unglamorous technical question our factory can actually settle: how sensitive is the ALS reversal ranking to LINCS context (cell line x dose x time)? If single-context rankings swing wildly for the SAME compound, exp001's output inherits arbitrary noise; if MODZ-aggregated, neural-weighted scoring stabilizes ranks while keeping positive controls sensible, the whole Stream B gets a defensible uncertainty measure (rank intervals, not point estimates). Pre-specifying kill criteria prevents post-hoc method shopping.
