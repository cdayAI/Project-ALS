## Rationale
UNC13A is one of the few ALS GWAS loci with a concrete, druggable-biology bridge to the core pathology: its toxic cryptic exon appears only when TDP-43 leaves the nucleus (TDP-43 proteinopathy is present in ~97% of cases, PMID 17023659, 40252666). If the GWAS signal colocalizes with the cryptic-exon sQTL, then genetic risk at this locus quantifies how much ALS burden flows through TDP-43 loss-of-function - converting the field's hardest target (TDP-43) into a measurable, stratifiable endpoint. That supports both (a) nominating spliceosome/RNA-processing nodes from the druggable genome near the locus, and (b) using carrier status or cryptic-exon burden as trial enrichment, analogous to SOD1 genotyping before tofersen.
