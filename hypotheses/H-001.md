## Rationale
exp001 scores compounds by cosine reversal against ONE bulk disease vector. Bulk ALS tissue conflates two opposite problems: neurons dying (neuronal genes DOWN) and glia activating (immune genes UP). A compound that "reverses the bulk signature" may do so by pushing on either axis or an artifact. Single-cell atlases let us split the bulk signature into cell-type-attributable components (regression/deconvolution with atlas-derived marker matrices), producing at least two orthogonal query vectors. We predict immune-axis reversal candidates will be enriched for known microglial/mast-cell modulators (the masitinib class, PMID 27400786) while neuronal-axis candidates enrich for membrane/excitability drugs, and that the two lists agree poorly (rho well below 0.9). If true, exp001's single ranking should be reported as context-conditional, not absolute.
