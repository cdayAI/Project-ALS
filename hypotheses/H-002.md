## Rationale
Signature-reversal wins in neuro came when signature logic was fused to a functional or clinical anchor (clemastine PMID 29029896; ropinirole PMID 37267913), and standalone CMap stories collapsed fast (bexarotene PMIDs 23764200, 26025659). The cheapest available human anchor for an immune/microglial mechanism in ALS is the CENTAUR observation that a (partially) active drug lowered YKL-40 and CRP (PMID 38050066). Hypothesis: compounds reversing the immune-dominant component of the ALS transcriptional signature are significantly enriched for perturbagens that also suppress YKL-40/CRP-axis genes in LINCS. If confirmed, the intersection set is the short list for any future iPSC-microglial co-culture assay (Answer ALS lines), giving each candidate a pre-named measurable correlate.
