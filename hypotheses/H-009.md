## Rationale
Masitinib works (if confirmed) in a clinically defined slice of patients nobody can identify biologically in advance; conversely, molecular subtypes exist (PMID 38129934) but have never been tied to actionable trial strata. If the immune/neuroinflammatory molecular subtype corresponds to the slow-'normal'-progressor phenotype that benefited from mast-cell/glial inhibition, then (a) the field gains a mechanism for enrichment success/failure patterns (edavarone and masitinib both needed subpopulations), and (b) a cheap clinical+blood proxy could replace expensive molecular profiling for enrollment. Testable end to end with open data: train subtype labels on Answer ALS expression, project onto blood-signature space, regress onto PRO-ACT clinical trajectories.
