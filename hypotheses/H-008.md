## Rationale
NfL is the field's best-validated dynamic biomarker, and the tofersen/ATLAS program already changed practice using NfL kinetics as a trigger (PMID 35585374). But no published work quantifies HOW MUCH trial power ML-modeled NfL trajectories buy versus conventional enrichment in the sporadic population where most trials enroll. PRO-ACT plus published NfL kinetic distributions allow a direct simulation: enroll-by-NfL-dynamics versus enroll-by-clinical-covariates versus no enrichment, comparing sample sizes at fixed power for realistic effect sizes (0.3-0.5 ALSFRS-R points/month). Given masitinib's enriched-cohort logic (PMIDs 31280619, 34457038) and the PHOENIX lesson that unenriched designs dilute signals, a validated NfL-dynamics enrichment recipe is immediately usable by trial designers and extends the qualified-biomarker story.
