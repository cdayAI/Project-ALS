## Rationale
Both falsified hypotheses died in underpowered line-pair iPSC datasets. Answer ALS offers what those datasets cannot: tens of C9orf72-expansion carriers with WGS-derived repeat sizes and bulk RNA-seq of differentiated spinal neurons, openly downloadable. This converts the central C9 question - does repeat burden drive a graded molecular phenotype? - into a regression problem with real statistical power rather than a two-point comparison. The LOF/transport axis is chosen as primary because it is the DPR-independent half of C9orf72 pathogenesis (drug-relevant by design: nuclear transport modulators exist), giving a target-id deliverable even if DPR biology itself stays intractable.

## Threshold derivation (pre-analysis)
Module-level MDE computed from measured residual SDs (see power_analysis block and
hypotheses/_LESSONS.md template): a ~100-300 gene module at n>=15/group detects mean shifts
of 0.10-0.20 log2 units at 80% power. Published LOF/transport effects in C9 neurons are
larger than this; therefore the bar is clearable IF AND ONLY IF the locked sample count
passes the go/no-go gate. Repeat-length gradient power: standardized slope >=0.45 detectable
at n~41 carriers.
